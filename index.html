Work Project / Lending Analytics

Pearth Loan Approval Modeling and EDA

A work analytics project using loan application data to understand approvals, cancellations, branch performance, loan-type behavior, and trend movement. The goal is to help stakeholders improve lending decisions, operational planning, and process review without exposing personal borrower details.

Clear Outcomes 18,782
Approval Rate 91.3%

Trend Analysis

The monthly trend separates application volume from approval-rate health. May 2025 shows a major demand spike while approval performance remains strong, which signals an opportunity to plan staffing and document review capacity before the branch network becomes overloaded.

KPI decision: monitor demand spikes, cancellation movement, and approval rate together. A rise in applications without a matching approval-rate drop can support scaling; a spike with more cancellations should trigger policy, documentation, or customer-targeting review.

Branch Decision Support

High-volume branches should be reviewed first because small workflow improvements can affect the most applications. Differences in approval rates can guide coaching, staffing, or process review.

Approval Drivers

Product-level approval patterns help the business see which loan types convert well and which ones may need clearer requirements, customer education, or risk-policy review.
